\begin{abstract}
Open-weight experts can be reused through parameter merging without the provider's permission. A useful release-time protection should preserve the expert's standalone function while disrupting its contribution after merging. We study a simple construction for CLIP vision encoders. Nullspace Bias Perturbation (\method) changes only the bias of the final LayerNorm. The added vector lies in the left nullspace of the expert's visual projection, so its contribution vanishes in the original encoder. Parameter merging changes this projection and can expose the perturbation. We choose the direction and magnitude from frozen weights and a fixed classifier, without images or optimization. A LayerNorm norm bound gives a sufficient condition for a constant prediction in a public-base interpolation model. In seven evaluated classification tasks, standalone accuracy changes by less than 0.03 percentage points, while protected-task accuracy after the evaluated AGA procedure is between 0.25\% and 9.80\%. We also report public MLP replacement and layer-wise distillation under explicit processing conditions. Direct modification of the final readout can remove the protection and is treated separately.
\end{abstract}
